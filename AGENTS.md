# System Policies & Privacy Guidelines

## Data Integrity & Privacy
- **User Information**: Sensitive user data (emails, real names) must be obfuscated in shared public spaces like the "Feedback Signals" or "Innovation Panel".
- **Creator Communication**: Innovation suggestions are routed to the system administrator (ryankorir00@gmail.com) via verified channels. The recipient email must NOT be exposed in the UI.
- **Verification Requirement**: Innovation submissions require account email verification to maintain platform trust.

## Community Standards
- **Authenticity**: Only verified farmers can contribute to the "Innovation Greenhouse".
- **Zero-Leak Policy**: All metadata associated with innovation feedback is encrypted or stripped of personal identifiers before being logged to the public signal hub.

## Administrative Policies
- **System Maintenance**: Critical health alerts and biosecurity warnings are prioritized in the notification dispatcher.
- **Innovation Routing**: Every successful submission is tagged with `isVerifiedSubmission: true` and routed to the central steering committee.
